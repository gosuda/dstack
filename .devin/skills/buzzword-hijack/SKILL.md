# Buzzword hijack

You ride a named jargon wave to position a product or message. The trend supplies the framing, never the facts. Every factual claim in the artifact must stand on its own with the jargon stripped out. Anything that is only true because of the buzzword gets rewritten or cut.

This is not trend analysis. If the user wants to understand a domain's jargon weather rather than execute a positioning move, say so and stop.

## What I need before I start

- The product, project, or message to position. It must be user supplied or source backed, never invented.
- The target surface: copy, landing page, or messaging. State it before writing anything.
- The jargon wave: the specific trending term or terms to ride. If the user has not named them, ask. Do not guess a wave.
- The output file path for the finished artifact.
- Evidence for the factual claims comes from user supplied material only. No social feed discovery happens here.
- Before producing a channel facing artifact, confirm the explicit target and get content consent.

## How the move gets built

1. Name the wave and the space it belongs to.
2. List every factual claim about the product, and mark which ones hold independently of the jargon. A claim that only holds because of the jargon is truth substitution: re-ground it or remove it.
3. Choose the surface the jargon enters (copy, landing, or messaging) and state the boundary plainly. Jargon lives in the positioning language (framing, headline, hook), never inside factual assertions.
4. Draft the artifact with the jargon in the framing and every factual claim self supporting without it.
5. Re-read the draft as an adversary. If any claim would be false or empty with the jargon removed, rewrite or cut it.
6. Write the finished artifact to the named local file, then report the file path, the chosen surface, the jargon term or terms used, the boundary where the jargon stops, and the factual claims confirmed to stand without the jargon.

## When I stop instead of shipping

- No factual claims were supplied and none can be grounded: stop and report the blocker rather than inventing claims.
- The trend truth check fails on some claims after drafting: keep the passing claims, report the failing ones, and do not write the final artifact until every claim is re-grounded or removed.
- Work here is reversible and local only. The only artifact is the named file, rolled back by deleting or reverting it. There is no publication, posting, outreach, campaign execution, or remote mutation, so nothing external ever needs rolling back.

## What you get

A local positioning artifact (copy, landing, or messaging) that rides the named wave, plus a report naming the file path, surface, jargon terms, the boundary where the jargon stops, and the factual claims verified to stand without the jargon.
