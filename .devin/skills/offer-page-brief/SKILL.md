# Offer page brief

Design an evidence-led offer or comparison page from confirmed facts, before final copy is written. You map the offer completely, formulate one proposition only from what the proof supports, and hand over a blueprint a writer or designer can build from. Nothing here invents, builds, or publishes.

This skill is read-only. It produces a blueprint and production requirements in chat, for handoff to `copywriting` or an approved design owner. It never implements the page, never publishes it, and never delivers final copy.

## What you need

- `offer` (required): category, deliverable, features and mechanisms, price and terms.
- `audience` (required): one primary audience in one situation. Multiple audiences mean separate briefs.
- `action` (required): the one principal action the page asks for. Multiple actions mean separate briefs.
- `proof` (required): supplied evidence, approved comparisons, available visual assets, eligibility and fit limits.

## Map the offer, then proposition it

Build the offer map with all ten slots filled or explicitly marked missing: category and deliverable; audience and situation; features and mechanisms; supported consequences; proof; price and terms; eligibility and fit limits; approved comparisons; available visuals; missing support. From the supported consequences alone, formulate one internal proposition containing no claim missing from the proof slot.

## Shape the page around buying questions

Select only page blocks that answer distinct buying questions (fit, mechanism, proof, terms, risk, next step) and drop decorative blocks, so each block names the buying question it answers. Every visual instruction must state what evidence it shows, never decoration: each visual carries an evidence caption requirement. State transaction terms and the actual next step in plain terms, including price, terms, eligibility, limits, and exclusions.

## Verify nothing is invented

Confirm no testimonial, result, urgency, scarcity, guarantee, price, or competitor claim is invented: every persuasion element traces to proof or is removed. Truthful urgency (real deadlines, real capacity) may appear only with its proof attached. When offer facts or proof are missing, produce the blueprint skeleton with the missing support flagged as production blockers instead of filling gaps with invention.

## What you hand back

A page blueprint in chat: the offer map, the internal proposition, the ordered page path with per-block buying questions, visual evidence requirements, terms and call to action specification, the missing-support list, and the verification record.
