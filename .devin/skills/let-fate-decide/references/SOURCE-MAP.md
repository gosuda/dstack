## Preserved constraints

- OS entropy by default (`secrets.randbelow` for both shuffles, `secrets.randbits` for reversals); no `os.urandom` direct use.
- Major and Minor decks shuffle independently; reversal flags are independent per card.
- Seeded runs replay the upstream draw order (Major shuffle, Minor shuffle, reversals in house order) through one isolated `random.Random` instance, are labeled in the output, and carry no entropy claim.
- Every draw verifies all 92 assets first (exit 2 naming every missing file); the skill never interprets from names alone.
- All 12 `ZODIAC_HOUSES` focus strings verified verbatim against the pinned house `**Domain**` lines (all 12 house files read at the pinned SHA).
- Interpretation limits preserved: hypothesis generation only, evidence decides safety/security/correctness, requirements override cards, one draw per decision, no redraw shopping, reading rides with the acting tool call.
